READOUT
Urgency words: urgent = expiring soon; hot = ahead of pace; watch = pressure; fine = room.
Disabled pools stay visible so absence is a verdict, not a missing row.
Enter / grok: 5h, 7d, monthly used, elapsed, reset, pace, and in-flight detail.
Enter / claude-code: 7d used, elapsed, reset and pace; no invented reset time.
Enter / command-code: monthly used, elapsed, reset and pace.
Enter / disabled: disabled reason; no licence meter is shown.
