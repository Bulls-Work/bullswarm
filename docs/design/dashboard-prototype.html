Bullswarm dashboard, working model

Click into the terminal and drive it: r b s y f switch pages, h opens help, 1–9 open a run, click any step, bar or value, ↑ ↓ PgUp PgDn and the wheel scroll under the sticky header and nav, Esc or ← goes back and then Home, p cycles the period, Tab cycles sub-tabs, ctrl+s copies the screen. Time runs eight times faster than real, so the two runs finish while you watch. Numbers are illustrative.
