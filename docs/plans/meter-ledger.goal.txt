Bullswarm 0.34.x: subscription spend attributed from a continuous meter ledger, so whole-percent meters still yield a measured share per attempt.

1. Every meter poll (live read or forced refresh) is appended to $BULLSWARM_HOME/meters/history/<pool>.jsonl with the provider timestamp, usedPct per window, resetsAt, the reading's resolution (1 for integer-percent providers such as codex, 0.1 for one-decimal providers such as claude-code and grok), and its source; the file is bounded and old rows roll off.
2. Each attempt's subscription share is computed from the ledger over its own [startedAt, finishedAt] window: positive interval deltas summed, a reset inside the window handled without a negative or double count, and a delta shared between concurrent attempts on the same pool in proportion to their API-rate cost; the record carries basis observed:meter-ledger, deltaPct, resolutionPct, and the ledger rows it used; the existing per-attempt snapshot path stays as the fallback and unknown stays null.
3. At run finish the attempts of one pool are reconciled so their shares sum to the pool's observed delta over the run (conservation), and rollups, Home, Run, Step and Stats show the ledger-based figures through the existing money-pair formatter with the new basis string.
4. Tests on real fixture histories (integer and decimal meters, an unchanged rounded reading, a reset, two concurrent attempts) prove the arithmetic, and npm test passes.
