Requirements:
1. One shared component module, src/workflow/stat-kit.js, is the single way a Stats surface is drawn: a panel with a title and rows, a horizontal share bar, a vertical column chart with axis and labels, a legend, and a summary card, each returning both its rendered lines and the hit regions of every bar it drew. Every chart on the Stats pages goes through it, the per-chart drawing code that stats-view.js carries today is deleted rather than left beside it, and additions to dash-kit.js are new exports only, changing no existing export's behaviour, because Budget and other pages depend on them.
2. The Spending tab: the spend-per-day column chart occupies the left half of the width and a two-by-two grid of breakdown panels occupies the right half; the chart's x axis labels name real dates a reader understands, never a bare weekday initial; a toggle switches the chart between stacking by pool and stacking by model, and the visible grid panels follow that choice; the fourth grid cell holds a panel the rollups can actually fill, and the report says which and why.
3. The Stats tabs are exactly Spending, Pool, Model and Project, each drawn with the same components in the same order and spacing, so the pages differ in their data and not in their layout; anything the retired Overview and Trends tabs showed that a reader still needs has a home on one of the four, and the report lists what moved where and what was dropped.
4. Every bar anywhere on the Stats pages is hoverable: a mouse move over a column, a stacked slice or a breakdown row shows a label naming the thing, its value and its share; a click pins the label until Escape or another click, so a tap works; the bars themselves are never recoloured or inverted on hover, and the label sits in a reserved row so the layout never jumps.
5. The pages render correctly at 55, 120 and 200 columns, stacking to one column when narrow, with no line over the width and no horizontal scrolling; text frames of the Spending tab at 55 and 120 columns are saved under docs/design/stats-frames-0.33.2/ from real rollup data.
