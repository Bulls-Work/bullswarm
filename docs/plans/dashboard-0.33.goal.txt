The 0.33.0 dashboard release in /Users/cowcow02/Repo/bullswork/bullswarm-dashboard: implement the approved prototype scope (docs/design/dashboard-prototype.html) with real data only. Out of scope: routing, steering, and the dispatch mechanism.

1. Record a per-run rollup when a run finishes, append it to a durable history index under ~/.bullswarm, record each run's project identity at goal time, and add `bullswarm workflow reindex` to backfill the runs that already exist, so day, period, pool, model and project totals never rescan every run directory.
2. Home shows today's tiles (finished, verified share, estimated spend, licence used), the 7-day breakdown by pool, model and project, the active runs always visible, and the recent list.
3. Budget shows every pool as a licence meter with its elapsed mark, the workflows/rest share bar, the biggest workflows, and money at the subscription rate and at the equivalent API rate, each labelled with its basis.
4. Stats has Overview, Trends with bar charts per metric over 7 days, 30 days and all time, plus Pools, Models and Projects, with clickable tiles and bars.
5. History is a per-date timeline of every workflow, newest first, loading older days as the reader scrolls.
6. Fleet shows the pools by lane and by provider, read-only, with edit opening setup.
7. Runs, Run and Step show the plan DAG, phase counts, ETA, per-step progress against expected, and pool share and spend figures.
8. Every key in `r b s y f h ? 1-9 Tab p Esc arrows PgUp PgDn Home End ctrl+s q` reaches its page or action, every tile, bar, run and step is clickable with the same action as its key, and Help names every one of them.
9. Every page renders in the phone layout at 55 columns with no painted line past the frame width.
10. The Claude Mod pane keeps working: `bullswarm workflow tui <id> --overview` and the six JSON commands keep their shapes, and usageLines stays exported.
11. Every money and licence figure on screen is measured or carries an `≈` and its stated basis; where no source exists the dashboard renders a blank and says why, never a number.
12. Tests under tests/ cover the new modules and every page at 120x40 and 55x26, and `npm test` passes.
13. docs/guide/observing.md, docs/reference/cli.md, README.md, src/help.js and mods/bullswarm/README.md describe the new pages, keys and commands.
14. CHANGELOG.md carries the 0.33.0 entry, including the key rebinding.
